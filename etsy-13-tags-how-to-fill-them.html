13 Etsy Tags: How to Fill All of Them (2026)

Updated 13 November 2026 · 8-minute read

Quick answer: Every Etsy listing gets 13 tags, each up to 20 characters long, and every empty tag is a search phrase you chose not to rank for. Fill all 13 with a mix of broad and long-tail phrases — product type, synonyms, material, style, recipient and occasion — and never repeat a phrase your title already covers. The formula and worked examples below fill a full set of 13 tags for three different products, so you can copy the pattern for your own listings. The rules first: what Etsy actually allows

Before the formula, the four rules that decide whether a tag works. Thirteen tags, twenty characters each. A tag is a phrase, not a single word, and the 20-character limit counts spaces — "personalised dog gift" is 21 characters and will not fit, so you would shorten it to "personal dog gift" at 17 characters. Count your characters before you save. Tags are search phrases. Etsy matches a buyer's search against your tags, so a tag should be something a real buyer would type: "boho wall decor", "dog memorial gift", "printable planner". Tags do not stack with the title. If a phrase is already in your title, putting it in a tag adds nothing — Etsy already knows the listing matches that phrase. Tags are not a place for your shop name, your username or the word "handmade" on its own. Nobody searches those words when they want to buy a product, and the slot is worth more as a buyer phrase. The 13-tag formula

Thirteen slots is a lot to fill from scratch, so use a formula. Think of the tags as six buckets, and fill each bucket in turn. Bucket 1 — the product itself (tags 1–2): the two most common names for the product, including the singular and plural where buyers search both ways, for example "dog portrait" and "pet portraits". Bucket 2 — synonyms (tags 3–4): other words buyers use for the same thing, for example "pet wall art" and "custom pet print". Bucket 3 — material and technique (tags 5–6): what it is made of and how it is made, for example "watercolour pet art" and "digital pet drawing". Bucket 4 — style (tags 7–8): the look and feel, for example "minimalist dog print" and "boho pet decor". Bucket 5 — recipient (tags 9–10): who the buyer is buying for, for example "gift for dog dad" and "pet lover gift". Bucket 6 — occasion and use (tags 11–13): when it is bought and why, for example "pet memorial gift", "dog loss sympathy gift" and "housewarming gift". That is 13 tags, and each one captures a different search — a different buyer, a different reason, a different set of words.

Worked example 1: a personalised necklace

Take a handmade birthstone necklace with a name engraving. A weak set of tags would be "necklace", "jewellery", "gift", "handmade", "cute" and eight more near-identical phrases — broad, single-word, and mostly wasted. The formula gives a far stronger set: "birthstone necklace", "name necklace", "personalised necklace", "custom name jewellery", "engraved necklace", "gold birthstone gift", "dainty necklace", "minimalist jewellery", "gift for mum", "birthday gift for her", "mothers day gift", "anniversary gift", "bridesmaid gift". Every tag is under 20 characters, every tag is a phrase a real buyer types, and no two tags repeat the same search. Notice that the broad tags ("name necklace") sit next to long-tail tags ("bridesmaid gift") — the broad ones bring volume, the long-tail ones bring buyers who know exactly what they want and convert better.

Worked example 2: a custom pet portrait print

Now a custom dog portrait print, the product from our earlier guide. The 13 tags: "dog portrait", "pet portraits", "custom pet print", "pet wall art", "watercolour pet art", "digital pet drawing", "minimalist dog print", "boho pet decor", "gift for dog dad", "pet lover gift", "pet memorial gift", "dog loss sympathy gift", "housewarming gift". Check each one against the rules. "Dog loss sympathy gift" is 22 characters — too long — so shorten it to "pet loss gift" or "sympathy pet gift" before saving. That is the kind of check the tag generator does automatically: it counts characters and flags any tag that will be cut off. Two tags in this set deserve a note. "Pet portraits" and "dog portrait" look similar, but buyers search both, and the plural is a genuinely different search. "Watercolour pet art" and "digital pet drawing" cover two different techniques — if the print is only watercolour, drop the digital tag and use a second style tag instead. Tags must be honest: a tag that describes a product you do not sell earns clicks that do not convert, and low conversion drags the listing down.

Worked example 3: a digital product

Digital products work the same way, and tags matter even more for them, because digital listings cannot rely on materials or shipping to stand out. Take a printable wedding planner: "wedding planner", "printable planner", "wedding planning kit", "bride planner pdf", "wedding binder", "printable wedding", "bride to be gift", "engagement gift", "wedding checklist", "day of timeline", "wedding organiser", "bridesmaid gift", "instant download". The last tag, "instant download", is worth noting: buyers of digital products search for it specifically, and it is one of the few tags that describes the format rather than the product. For digital listings, one format tag — "instant download", "printable pdf", "digital download" — is almost always worth a slot.

Where to find tag ideas for your own products

You do not need a paid tool to fill 13 tags. Start with the Etsy search bar: type your product and read the autocomplete suggestions — those are real searches from real buyers, ranked by volume. Type the first two words of a tag and watch what Etsy suggests next; that is how you find the exact long-tail phrases buyers use. Then look at the top 10 listings for your main keyword and write down the words their titles share — the overlap is the vocabulary Etsy already rewards for that product. The listings themselves do not show their tags, but their titles and first lines are a strong guide to the phrases that rank. Finally, expand in the six directions from the formula: synonyms, materials, styles, recipients, occasions and formats. Seasonal tags: the slots that should rotate

Not all 13 tags need to stay the same all year. Two or three slots should rotate with the seasons, because a tag that earns its place in November wastes it in June. "Christmas dog gift" is a strong tag in November and December; in June it should become "summer birthday gift" or "fathers day gift". The same applies to events: "mothers day gift" in April, "graduation gift" in May, "valentines gift" in January. Plan the rotation in advance — a simple note of which tags change in which month — and change only the seasonal slots, leaving the other ten or eleven tags stable so you can see what the change actually did. Change tags one listing at a time where possible, and wait two to four weeks before judging the result, because Etsy re-indexes listings gradually.

Mistakes that waste tag slots

  • Fewer than 13 tags — every empty tag is a search phrase you gave up.
  • Tags copied from the title — tags should capture new searches, not repeat old ones.
  • One-word tags like "gift", "cute" or "pretty" — too broad to rank and too vague to convert.
  • Near-identical tags — "dog gift", "dog gifts", "gift for dog" and "dog lover gift" are four slots spent on one search.
  • Tags over 20 characters — Etsy cuts them off, so a tag that reads well in your notes may be broken on the listing.
  • Tags that describe a product you do not sell — clicks without conversions hurt the listing.
  • Your shop name or username as a tag — nobody searches for it, and the slot is worth more as a buyer phrase.
  • Never updating tags — refresh the seasonal slots every few months and keep the versions that improve clicks.

Frequently asked questions

How many tags can you use on an Etsy listing?
You get 13 tags per listing, and each tag can be up to 20 characters long. Use all 13. Every empty tag is a search phrase you chose not to rank for, and Etsy's search reads tags as a major relevance signal alongside your title.

Should Etsy tags repeat words from the title?
No. Your title already covers those words, so repeating them in tags wastes the slot. Tags should capture different searches: synonyms, materials, styles, recipients and occasions that the title did not have room for.

Can one Etsy tag be more than one word?
Yes — a tag is a phrase, not a single word. A multi-word tag like "custom dog gift" counts as one tag and matches the exact phrase buyers type. Multi-word tags are almost always more valuable than single words like "gift" or "dog".

What happens if an Etsy tag is longer than 20 characters?
Etsy will not accept it. The tag field stops at 20 characters, so a phrase that is too long gets cut off or rejected. Shorten the phrase before you save it — drop a filler word or use a shorter synonym rather than letting Etsy truncate it mid-word.
